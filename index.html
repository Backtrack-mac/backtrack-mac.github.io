Backtrack for Mac

Backtrack logs every keystroke across all your apps—except passwords—along with window names, timestamps, and screenshots of the active screen. All data is securely stored in a fast SQLite database for effortless retrieval and review.

Complete Activity Record

Capture exactly what was typed, where, and when. Window titles and app names provide full context, turning scattered inputs into a precise timeline of your work.

Visual Context

Each entry includes a screenshot of the topmost window or desktop. See not just the text, but the exact interface state at the moment of input.

Lightning-Fast Search

Query your SQLite database instantly. Filter by app, window, date, or keywords to reconstruct sessions or recover lost work in seconds.

Privacy-First Design

Passwords are automatically excluded. Local storage ensures your data stays on your Mac, with no cloud dependencies or external access.

Seamless Integration

Runs quietly in the background across all applications. Version 2.5.4 includes refined UX and bug fixes for reliable, unobtrusive performance.
